# EduSight — Algorithmic Governance & Model Policy

## 1. Scope & Objective
This policy defines the standards for training, evaluating, deploying, and interpreting machine learning models within EduSight.

---

## 2. Leakage Prevention Protocol
1. **Target Feature Exclusion**: The native `Target` column and the derived binary label `at_risk` are strictly excluded from all training and feature matrices.
2. **Temporal Window Integrity**:
   - Week 4 models cannot include 1st or 2nd semester internal course grades.
   - Week 8 models cannot include 2nd semester course grades.
   - Any feature whose availability timestamp succeeds the prediction window cutoff is categorized as a critical leakage violation and triggers immediate pipeline failure.
3. **Data Preprocessing Isolation**:
   - StandardScaler, imputer, and normalizers must be fit exclusively on training folds.
   - SMOTE must be encapsulated inside `imblearn.pipeline.Pipeline` folds. Applying SMOTE before splitting is forbidden.

---

## 3. Evaluation Standards & Threshold Strategy
1. **Separation of Test Set**: Test sets (held out 20%) are reserved solely for final evaluation. Test sets are never utilized for hyperparameter tuning or threshold selection.
2. **Asymmetric Cost Thresholding**: Because failing to support an at-risk student is significantly more detrimental than a false alert, decision thresholds are optimized with a 5:1 False Negative penalty weight on cross-validation probabilities.
3. **Mandatory Reporting Metrics**: Models must report Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, and Brier Score.

---

## 4. Explainability & Language Constraints
- Models must provide local explanations via SHAP TreeExplainer.
- Generated textual explanations must convey statistical associations, never deterministic or causal claims.
- The terms *"will fail"*, *"doomed"*, or *"caused failure"* are prohibited. Recommended phrasing: *"Feature X contributed to the elevated predicted risk score."*

---

## 5. Recourse Feasibility Rules
- Counterfactual recommendations generated by DiCE must never alter immutable demographics (Gender, Age, Nationality, Parental Qualifications).
- Recommended targets must be mathematically reachable within remaining course credits.
- All recommendations carry the advisory notice: *"Please discuss these model-based recommendations with your academic advisor before making changes."*
